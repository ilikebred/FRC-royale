The Net remains an unupgradable premium storage option at 80 fuel. Level-1 dual turret is retained for existing equipment/save compatibility. Max single OTB retains its full-width suction behavior. Drivetrain, weight, and climber upgrades are unchanged.

Rates are before boosts, defense, accuracy and opponent skill modifiers. Fixed shooters must settle and face the alliance pass target when feeding; turrets can feed while moving. Auton estimates include fixed-shooter feed alignment time.
